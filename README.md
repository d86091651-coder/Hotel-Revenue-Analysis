🏨 Hotel Revenue Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Hotel Revenue Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI. The dashboard analyzes booking, revenue, customer segmentation, cancellation behavior, and forecasting trends from 2023 to 2026.

The goal of this project is to provide actionable insights to Revenue Managers and General Managers for improving pricing strategies, reducing cancellations, and optimizing revenue.

📊 Dashboard Highlights

🔹 Core KPIs

Total Revenue: 1.07 Bn+

Occupancy Rate: 22.42%

ADR: 4.48K

RevPAR: 1.00K

Room Nights Sold: 240K

Cancellation Rate: 25.26%

📈 Key Analytical Sections

1️⃣ Revenue Analysis

Yearly and Monthly Revenue Trend

Revenue by Room Category

Revenue by City

Revenue by Booking Channel

Upsell Potential By Booking Count

2️⃣ Occupancy & Forecasting

Occupancy % Trend

Revenue vs Occupancy Relationship

Seasonal Occupancy Impact

3️⃣ Customer Segmentation

Loyal vs Regular vs First-Time Guests

Nationality-wise Revenue Distribution

Stay Duration Analysis

Stay Purpose Analysis

4️⃣ Booking Channel Insights

Revenue by Booking Channel

Cancellation by Booking Channel

Channel Preference by Stay Type

5️⃣ Cancellation & Refund Analytics

Cancellation Trend (Monthly)

Cancellation Rate by Stay Purpose

Cancelled vs Completed Comparison

Revenue Lost Due to Cancellation

Refund Trend Analysis

6️⃣ Upsell & Revenue Optimization

Upsell Potential by Stay Type (Spa & Dining)

Revenue by Season × Room Category

🧮 DAX Measures Used

Aggregation & Logical Measures

Room Category

Customer Segment

🎯 Business Insights

Premium rooms generate highest revenue.

Cancellation rate (~25%) significantly impacts revenue stability.

Revenue is evenly distributed across booking channels.

Loyal & regular guests contribute majority of revenue.

Peak season drives highest cancellation count.

Upselling opportunities exist in long-stay bookings.
